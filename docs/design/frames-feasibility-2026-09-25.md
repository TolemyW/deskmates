## Grader and controls

`spikes/reusable-tasks/frames/prepare.py` pins the TSV, row hashes, page revision IDs, and page-content hashes. `grade.py` separately reports `exact_answer` (an `Answer: <integer>` line compared to the private row), `source_integrity` (diagnostic snapshot validation), `citation_structure` (diagnostic URL membership in the frozen corpus), and `citation_support` (factual support of each hop). URL membership never produces a support pass. It does not require the bot to cite every hidden benchmark URL: a valid alternative source subset remains structurally acceptable and goes to factual review. `citation-review.md` defines independent hop labels, passage locations, two human reviewers, and adjudication. **No two-human calibration has occurred**; an AI review or URL match does not satisfy that gate. Support remains `unknown` for both cases.

## Boundary before a model trial

The browser adapter must serve an attributed frozen corpus in an isolated workspace, prevent live-web drift with a declared egress allowlist, and retain fetched-page hashes and trace. A later fixture design should avoid making a five-page curated list itself the answer path; index/search behavior and distractors need preflight. The evaluator must not mount `row-*.json`, the TSV, source-link lists, manifests, or grader controls into the bot workspace. Citation reviewers must calibrate positive and negative examples and record per-hop labels before any support score. Row 2 needs a documented decision on its ambiguous premise. Neither case has a frozen model budget or an executed Deskmates attempt.
